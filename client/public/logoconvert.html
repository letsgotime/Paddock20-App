Auth0 Requirements

Auth0 requires a logo URL that:

  • Uses HTTPS (upload the PNG to a service like GitHub, Cloudinary, or Imgur)
  • Is publicly accessible
  • Has a transparent background
  • Recommended dimensions: 150x50 pixels minimum
